WHY OWNERS SWITCH

Built around how a Tamil Nadu counter actually runs

Every feature below is live in the app today — not a roadmap promise.

அ

Tamil on the paper bill, not just the screen

Most billing software only shows Tamil on-screen. Item names print correctly in Tamil on the customer's bill and the kitchen ticket — no garbled characters.

🖨

Print from whatever's at the counter

Bills and kitchen tickets print straight from a counter PC, a tablet, or your cashier's own Android phone — no special drivers, no dedicated hardware.

₹

GST shown the way customers expect

CGST and SGST always print as two separate lines with Indian-style ₹ formatting — no merged "GST" line that makes a customer double-check.

🏷

Offers apply themselves

Set a festival offer or item discount once — it applies automatically at billing. No cashier math, no under- or over-discounting at the till.

🧑‍🍳

Know who's actually selling

Waiter and cashier incentive tracking is built in, with a ready-made payout worksheet — no separate spreadsheet at month-end.

📶

Tolerant of patchy WiFi

Orders save as staff enter them, so a weak signal at the till doesn't cost you a held bill or a lost ticket to the kitchen.
